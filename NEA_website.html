Helpful information

How do I use the EEG Brain State Predictor?

The model allows you to view quantitatively how well you slept the night before, enabling our users to more deeply understand how to get to sleep. Simply enter your EEG file from your headset your results will be generated in seconds.

How do I understand my results?

Our model allows you to classify 5 sleep stages: Awake, NREM Sleep Stage 1, NREM Sleep Stage 2, NREM Sleep Stage 3/4, and REM sleep. REM stands for Rapid Eye Movement and marks the period of main dreaming activity, characterised by full body paralysis excluding the eyes which move frequently.

Biography

Hi, I am Thomas Delfino, creator of EEG Sleep State predictor. The average human spends roughly 25 years of their life asleep. However, the majority of people don't regard it with as much importance as it should be. Sleep impacts our health, happiness and general quality of life so we should treat treat our sleep like a priority, and reclaim the decades you spend in the dark.
